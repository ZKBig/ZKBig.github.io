I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Minjia Zhang. Prior to UIUC, I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yingyan (Celine) Lin at the EIC Lab as a Research Assistant in the School of Computer Science, Georgia Tech.

My research focuses on efficient and interpretable foundation models. Specifically: (1) leveraging sparsity and system–algorithm co-design to accelerate the inference and training of LLMs and VLMs; (2) developing empirical and theoretical insights to understand and optimize LLMs and VLMs.

Outside of research, I like to stay healthy by working out regularly, and I'm really into playing 🎾 tennis — it keeps me both physically and mentally sharp.
